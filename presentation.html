Key Data Insights

  • 🕰️ Call Duration: The strongest single predictor. Longer calls indicate genuine engagement and higher conversion.
  • 💼 Demographics: Retired clients (>50% rate) and students (74% rate) are the most receptive demographics.
  • 🔄 Previous Success: Clients with a successful prior campaign convert at over 60%.
  • 📅 Seasonality: May has the highest contact volume but low conversion. March, Sep, Oct, and Dec are highly effective.
  • 📱 Contact Method: Cellular contact significantly outperforms landlines.

Task 1: Feature Engineering

Created 4 new features to improve predictive signal before modeling:

has_previous

1 if contacted in prior campaign

contacted_before

1 if pdays ≠ -1

balance_log

Log-transformed account balance

age_group

Age binned into 5 ordinal groups

Model Pipeline

Preprocessing: 80/20 Stratified Split, Label Encoding, StandardScaler (for Linear models)

4 Models Evaluated:

  • Logistic Regression (Linear Baseline)
  • Decision Tree (Non-linear rules)
  • Random Forest (Bagging Ensemble)
  • Gradient Boosting (Sequential Boosting)

Business Recommendations

Based on our models, the marketing team should:

  • Prioritize Duration: Train agents to sustain meaningful conversations.
  • Target Successes: Clients with previous campaign success are the highest-quality leads.
  • Shift Timing: Reduce May outreach; focus heavily on March, Sep, Oct.
  • Target Demographics: Specifically tailor campaigns for retired clients and students.
  • Update Contact Info: Cellular contact is crucial; prioritize gathering mobile numbers.

Conclusion

The Gradient Boosting classification pipeline predicts term deposit subscriptions with 89.4% accuracy and an AUC of 0.96.

By deploying this model as a lead-scoring tool, the bank can optimize call times, reduce campaign fatigue, and significantly increase conversion rates.
